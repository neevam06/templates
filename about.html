It started with a simple block party.

Founded in 2021, Carnivalo was born out of a desire to reconnect neighbors after years of distance. What began as a small tool to help manage a neighborhood street fair in Rio has grown into a global platform.

Today, we empower organizers—from small town committees to major event production companies—to create unforgettable experiences. We handle the logistics, ticketing, and promotion so you can focus on the magic.

Our Core Values

We're guided by a set of principles that ensure every event we touch is safe, inclusive, and undeniably fun.

Community First

Events aren't just about entertainment; they're about belonging. We prioritize local connection in everything we build.

Unbridled Joy

Life is too short for boring events. We design for delight, surprise, and those "wow" moments that become memories.

Safety & Trust

Fun requires peace of mind. We provide robust tools for crowd management, ticketing security, and verified organizers.
